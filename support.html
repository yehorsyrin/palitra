Common questions

The camera shows a crossed-out icon. Palitra has no camera access. Open Settings → Palitra and turn Camera on.

«Save Image» is missing in the share sheet. Update to the latest version of Palitra.

How do I delete a work? Touch and hold any work in the grid, tap the bin, then confirm.

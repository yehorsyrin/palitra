Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Palitra does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Photos and camera

Photos you take or pick are processed on your device to find their colours. They are saved only inside the app on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Saving to Photos

Palitra asks for permission only to add finished images to your photo library, and only when you choose to save one.

No accounts, analytics or ads

There is no sign-in, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. The app works without an internet connection.

Contact

Questions about privacy: github.com/yehorsyrin/palitra/issues
